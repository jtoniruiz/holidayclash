Common questions

Where does the holiday data come from?

HolidayClash uses the open-source Nager.Date public holiday API, which aggregates official government data for 100+ countries and is continuously updated.

Is HolidayClash really free?

Yes. The tool is free to use without registration. We may show display ads to cover hosting costs, but core functionality is free forever.

Do you cover regional or state-level holidays?

For most countries we cover national public holidays. Some countries also include regional subdivisions where the data is officially published. Always double-check region-specific holidays for legally critical decisions.

Can I export the calendar?

An export feature (CSV and iCal) is on the roadmap. For now you can view holidays year-by-year in the tool above.
