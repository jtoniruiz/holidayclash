Contact us

Got feedback, a bug to report, or a feature request? We'd love to hear from you.

Email:

info@holidayclash.com

We aim to respond within 5 business days.

What to reach out about

Data corrections. If you spot an incorrect or missing public holiday, please note that we source data from the open-source Nager.Date API. Corrections should ideally be reported at their GitHub issues page so all downstream users benefit. You can also let us know and we'll flag it upstream.

Feature suggestions. Things you'd love HolidayClash to do next? Frequently requested: iCal export, historical data, more country coverage, custom notifications. Tell us what would make this more useful for your team.

Bug reports. If something looks broken or doesn't work as expected, describe what you did, what you expected to see, and what actually happened. Include your browser and device if possible.

Partnership or licensing. If you'd like to use HolidayClash data or embed our tool in your product, get in touch.

Advertising and business inquiries. For advertising partnerships or business-related questions, please write us with details of your request.

Anything else. Say hi. We read every message.
